You have a card in progress — didn't mean to close it?

1 · Game title

Leave it as the default or make it your own — like "Joel's BUSTD Bingo". It centers and shrinks to fit automatically.

2 · Your phrase pool

Flip a phrase off to keep it out of cards without deleting it. Tap ✎ to change the wording, add a description (adds an ⓘ on the square), or turn it into a ⭐ Bonus Square. Tap ✕ to remove it for good.

3 · Card size

⭐ Bonus rewards
Each card draws a random, unique mix from your enabled phrases, so no two are alike. Quick 4×4 has no center square (even grids don't have one) — Classic 5×5 and Mini 3×3 can both use the free space if you want it.

Bonus squares per card keeps every player's card fair; it's capped by how many ⭐ Bonus Squares you have turned on (set it to 0 for none).

Bonus rewards sets how Bonus Squares behave: Default uses the reward type set on each phrase, Random makes every one roll a surprise reward (Pick, Swap, or Search) at claim time, and None turns bonuses off entirely for a straight game.

4 · Winning patterns

Pick which shapes count as a win, on top of a full blackout card (blackout always wins). Choose as many as you like — a card wins as soon as any one selected shape is fully dabbed. Mini 3×3 is too small for shape patterns, so it always wins on Any Line.

Heads up: Any Line needs the fewest dabs of any shape, so if it's selected alongside a bigger shape (Frame, X, Plus, Siren Bars, etc.), Any Line will almost always win first — the bigger shape rarely gets to be the deciding one. Deselect Any Line if you want a specific shape to matter.

5 · Do something with it

How do I get cards onto other people's devices?
  • Text a link (group play): tap Share a unique card. Everyone who opens it gets their own unique card drawn from your pool — exactly how bingo should work. This needs the page to live at a web address (see below).
  • Let someone build their own game: tap Share game setup instead. They land on the Setup screen with your pool and settings pre-loaded, so they can tweak the pool or size before generating their own card — good for handing off hosting duties.
  • Email with no setup: just email this bingo.html file as an attachment. Whoever opens it on their device gets the full app offline — they build or play cards right there, nothing to host.
  • To make link-sharing work: this one file has to be hosted somewhere public and free — GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or Netlify all work. Drop the file, get a URL like yoursite/bingo.html, and every share link points back to it. Ask me and I'll walk you through whichever host you like.
