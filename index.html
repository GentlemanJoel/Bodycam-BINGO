Build a word pool → watch the footage → dab your card → call BUSTD.

1 · Your word pool

Flip a phrase off to keep it out of cards without deleting it. Tap ✎ to change the wording, add a description (adds an ⓘ on the square), or turn it into a ⭐ Bonus Square. Tap ✕ to remove it for good.

2 · Card size

Each card draws a random, unique mix from your enabled phrases, so no two are alike. Quick 4×4 has no center square (even grids don't have one) — Classic 5×5 and Mini 3×3 can both use the free space if you want it.

3 · Winning patterns

Pick which shapes count as a win, on top of a full blackout card (blackout always wins). Choose as many as you like — a card wins as soon as any one selected shape is fully dabbed. Only available for Classic 5×5 and Quick 4×4.

4 · Do something with it

How do I get cards onto other people's phones?

Text a link (group play): tap Share game link. Everyone who opens it gets their own unique card they can tap to dab — exactly how bingo should work. This needs the page to live at a web address (see below).

Email with no setup: just email this bingo.html file as an attachment. Whoever opens it on their phone gets the full app offline — they build or play cards right there, nothing to host.

To make link-sharing work, this one file has to be hosted somewhere public and free — GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or Netlify all work. Drop the file, get a URL like yoursite/bingo.html, and every share link points back to it. Ask me and I'll walk you through whichever host you like.
